# Figure data sources

These coordinate files are digitised from published figures; they are not the
original experimental measurements.

- `Fig2_Zeff_etal2000.mat`: Fig. 2 of Zeff, Kleber, Fineberg and Lathrop,
  *Nature* **403**, 401–404 (2000), doi:10.1038/35000151. The notebook labels
  the columns as `t - t0` [ms] and cavity length [cm].
- `Fig4_Zeff_etal2000.dat`: Fig. 4 of the same paper. Columns are height [cm]
  and velocity [m/s].
- `Fig5a_Cattaneo2025.dat`: Fig. 5(a) of Cattaneo, Presse and Supponen,
  *Physical Review Letters* **135**, 204002 (2025), doi:10.1103/7c4p-y9jr.
  Columns are wave amplitude [µm] and jet velocity [m/s].

The comparison notebook records the calculations and plotted quantities.
Reuse of digitised points and paper figure assets must respect the cited
publications' terms and include their attribution.
